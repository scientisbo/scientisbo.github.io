# About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Robotics Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

My current interests include robot control, robot system engineering, mobile robot navigation, embedded control, and embodied intelligence.

I am especially interested in building reliable robotic systems that connect algorithms, software architecture, control, simulation, and real-world deployment.

# Technical Interests

- Robot system integration and debugging
- C++ and ROS-based robotic software development
- Motion control and trajectory tracking
- Mobile robot navigation and dynamic obstacle avoidance
- Embedded systems and motor control
- Simulation-to-real robotic system development

# Selected Projects

- UR5 Rehabilitation Robot Admittance Control and Trajectory Tracking Simulation
- Mobile Robot Spatial Interaction Navigation and Target Following System
- Optimization Algorithm Experiments and Research
- Intelligent Recognition System for Hydrogen Trapping Information in Metals

# Research Experience

I have participated in research work related to optimization algorithms, robotic simulation, trajectory tracking, navigation, and intelligent recognition systems.
